# Arkhe Multiomics

**Bioinformatics Analysis · Reproducible Workflows · Scientific Consulting · Specialized Training**

## From complex omics data to meaningful biological insights.

Arkhe Multiomics provides specialized computational analysis, reproducible workflows, and scientific support for single-cell, spatial, and integrated multiomics research.

[Discuss your project](mailto:elarkhe@gmail.com?subject=Bioinformatics%20project%20inquiry) · [Explore our expertise](#scientific-expertise)

---

## What we do

### Bioinformatics analysis

End-to-end and targeted analysis, from data quality control and preprocessing to statistical analysis, visualization, and biological interpretation.

### Pipelines and reproducible workflows

Development, adaptation, evaluation, and documentation of computational workflows suited to research questions, data characteristics, and project requirements.

### Scientific consulting

Support for study planning, analytical strategy, method selection, troubleshooting, and collaborative computational research.

### Specialized training

Practical workshops and tailored instruction in bioinformatics methods, sequencing-data analysis, and reproducible research.

## Scientific expertise

| Area | Example analyses |
| --- | --- |
| **Single-cell RNA-seq** | Quality control, cell annotation, clustering, differential expression, and transcriptional heterogeneity. |
| **Single-cell ATAC-seq** | Chromatin accessibility, cell-type-specific regulatory regions, and motif analysis. |
| **Spatial transcriptomics** | Spatial domains, tissue organization, expression patterns, and cell-type mapping. |
| **Multiomics integration** | Integrated RNA, chromatin accessibility, and spatial analyses to investigate cellular and regulatory relationships. |
| **Bulk RNA-seq** | Quality control, quantification, differential expression, and functional interpretation. |

Analyses and deliverables are scoped to each project's scientific questions, experimental design, and data availability.
