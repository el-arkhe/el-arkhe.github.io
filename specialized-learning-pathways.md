  <section class="arkhe-section arkhe-soft" aria-labelledby="pathway-modules">
    <p class="arkhe-eyebrow">MODULAR ACADEMIC OFFER</p><h2 id="pathway-modules">Four specialized modules</h2>
    <p class="arkhe-section-intro">Each module is designed as a 40-hour learning experience, combining conceptual foundations, guided analysis, and applied work with curated datasets.</p>
    <div class="arkhe-grid arkhe-grid-2">
      <article class="arkhe-card"><span class="arkhe-number">M2 · 40 HOURS</span><h3>Single-cell gene expression · scRNA-seq</h3><p>Reproducible transcriptomic analysis, cell heterogeneity, annotation, and biological interpretation.</p></article>
      <article class="arkhe-card"><span class="arkhe-number">M3 · 40 HOURS</span><h3>Single-cell chromatin accessibility · scATAC-seq</h3><p>Chromatin accessibility, regulatory regions, transcription-factor signals, and cellular identity.</p></article>
      <article class="arkhe-card"><span class="arkhe-number">M4 · 40 HOURS</span><h3>Integration · scRNA-seq + scATAC-seq</h3><p>Connect transcriptomic and epigenomic profiles to study cell states and gene regulation.</p></article>
      <article class="arkhe-card"><span class="arkhe-number">M5 · 40 HOURS</span><h3>Spatial transcriptomics</h3><p>Analyze spatial gene expression, tissue organization, and the relationship between molecular identity and spatial context.</p></article>
    </div>
    <p class="arkhe-note">The academic pathway is designed primarily for asynchronous learning, complemented by academic support and live sessions. Module availability and institutional implementation are discussed individually.</p>
